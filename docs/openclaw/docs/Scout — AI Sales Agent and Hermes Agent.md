## About

Scout is a pre-configured, production-ready sales persona that plugs directly into your OpenClaw environment. Download the config files, fill in your ICP and sales context, deploy, and Scout starts researching leads and building pipeline immediately.

You are not starting from scratch. Scout is designed to give you a working outbound system for lead research, cold email sequences, CRM updates, and follow-up tracking on day one.

## What Scout handles out of the box

- **Lead research & qualification** — finds prospects matching your ICP, scores them, and surfaces the best opportunities.
- **Cold email sequences via Instantly** — writes personalized outreach in your voice, loads sequences, and manages send schedules.
- **CRM sync (HubSpot/Pipedrive/Notion)** — keeps your pipeline updated automatically so nothing falls through the cracks.
- **Follow-up tracking** — never let a lead go cold. Scout monitors replies, books follow-ups, and escalates hot prospects.
- **Pipeline reporting** — daily and weekly reports on open rates, reply rates, meetings booked, and pipeline value.

## FAQ

### Do I need coding experience?

No. You download files, fill in your sales details, and paste into OpenClaw.

### What tools does Scout work with?

Instantly for email sequences, HubSpot, Pipedrive, or Notion for CRM, Apify for lead scraping, Gmail, and Telegram for notifications.

### Which AI model works best?

Scout works with any model supported by OpenClaw. Claude and GPT-4 both perform well.

### Scout vs Atlas — which should I buy?

Scout is purpose-built for sales pipeline. Atlas is a general-purpose executive operator. If your main job is outbound sales, Scout. If you need outreach plus content plus inbox plus operations, Atlas. Many people use both.

### Can I customize Scout?

Yes — every file is editable. Adjust the SOUL.md, change the outreach style, modify the daily rhythm, or add your own skills.

### Does this work with Hermes Agent?

Yes. Every first-party persona and skill on Remote OpenClaw works with both OpenClaw and Hermes Agent. The package is delivered through the Remote OpenClaw workflow, but the operating pattern is portable across both stacks.
