## Executive Summary

The most successful AI travel companies do **not** make money from trip planning.

They make money from:

1. Hotel bookings
2. Flights
3. Activities
4. Travel packages
5. Premium subscriptions
6. Sponsored placements
7. Enterprise licensing
8. Affiliate commissions

The AI planner is a **customer acquisition engine**.

The booking ecosystem is the **revenue engine**.
