# Most Important UX Insight

## Mindtrip architecture:

```text
AI Chat
  ↓
Persistent Trip
  ↓
Structured Travel Data
  ↓
Timeline + Maps + Collections
```

The trip becomes:

```text
a living database
```

not just a chat.
