# Final Recommendation

## Best strategy

### Use:

- CopilotKit → contestant experience
    
- Mastra → workflows + orchestration
    
- Supabase → deterministic truth
    
- Gemini → coaching + content
    
- ADK → venue/location intelligence
    

---

## Most important principle

The AI should feel like:

```text
a pageant mentor
a manager
a coordinator
a coach
```

not:

- a judge
    
- an autonomous decision-maker
    
- a voting authority
    
- a winner selector
